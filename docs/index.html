How it plays

Gates

Math gates you can shoot

+, −, × and ÷ gates change your squad size. Shoot a gate to improve it — bad gates shrink and can flip to +1.

Traps

Dodge the barricades

Run into a trap and it costs you soldiers. Steer with a swipe, the mouse, or A/D.

Enemies

Five kinds of undead

Walkers, fast skeletons, armoured tanks, ranged vampires that throw dodgeable shots, and a gold ghost worth 15 coins.

Loot

Crates & guns

Shoot crates for extra soldiers or a gun upgrade: Pistol → SMG → Rifle → Gatling.

Skills

Frost, Shock, Inferno

Freeze the horde, zap everything nearby, or burn the road ahead.

Physics

Things fly

Defeated zombies tumble as physics bodies and crates burst into debris.

Install on Android

  1. Download one of the APKs above on your phone.
  2. Open it. Android asks to allow installs from your browser or Files app — allow it once.
  3. Tap Install. If Play Protect warns about an unknown developer, choose Install anyway (the app is self-signed, not on Google Play).

Tested on an Android 11 emulator, not yet on a physical phone. No permissions, no ads, no network use. Test notes.
